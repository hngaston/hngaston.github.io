I am a second-year Computer Science and Data Science student at Caltech, where I am advised by Professor Anima Anandkumar. My current research focuses on developing models that analyze Earth data to monitor natural and built environments. I am excited about projects where I can continue to apply AI for good.

My academic and research experiences have created a strong interest in applying machine learning to meaningful and challenging problems in areas such as climate change, medicine, disaster response, and defense. I am particularly excited about roles where I can help develop technology to directly address meaningful problems in these domains and others.

Currently, I’m working as the David C. Elliot SURF fellow at Caltech under Professor Hannah Druckenmiller. I am collaborating with UC Berkeley postdoc Ritwik Gupta to build a transformer model that detects new construction from the latent space of geospatial foundation models, like AlphaEarth. Our goal is to quantify Clean Water Act land use regulation non-compliance and analyze how land use deregulation affects new construction patterns on a nation-wide scale. I am also working with Professor Georgia Gkioxari to develop vision models that track environmental change over time and predict their impact.

Previously, I worked with Professor Zhiwei Feng at the University of Pittsburgh to develop a computational pipeline for antibody redesign. Through my research experiences, I have developed a strong foundation, and a growing interest, in applying modern machine learning and computer vision techniques to purposeful challenges.

I am currently seeking internship opportunities for Summer 2026.
