I'm an undergraduate at Caltech studying Computer Science and Data Science, where I am academically advised by Professor Anima Anandkumar. I am passionate about researching and applying Machine Learning and Computer Vision to real-world problems.

This summer I'm interning at Smith + Nephew Robotics, where I'm building agents for intra-operative robot-surgeon interaction with the CORI orthopedic surgery robot.

At Caltech, I research with Professor Hannah Druckenmiller as the David C. Elliot SURF fellow, collaborating with University of Maryland Professor Ritwik Gupta to build transformer models that detect new construction from the latent space of geospatial foundation models like AlphaEarth and TESSERA. Our goal is to quantify Clean Water Act non-compliance and study how land-use deregulation shapes development across the country.

Previously, I worked with Professor Zhiwei Feng at the University of Pittsburgh on a computational pipeline for antibody redesign.

I'm broadly excited about machine learning and computer vision for high-impact domains such as medicine, climate, disaster response, etc., and about building technology that addresses real problems directly.
